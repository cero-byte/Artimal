A way to slow down, notice what you're feeling,
and turn it into something you can see.
No drawing experience needed.
Grounded in research. The system is built on a quantitative study of 848 adolescents (ages 13–17) participating in art therapy. Every step was designed around what the research actually found helps people across all artistic skill levels.

2 Link your words with imagination
3 Go deeper with your image

This isn't a replacement for professional mental health support — but based on our findings, a good therapeutic art-making outcome doesn't depend on artistic skill level.
